# Code of Conduct

LATTICEWORK is an evidence-first engineering project.

## Expected conduct

Participants must:

- Critique work without attacking people.
- Support technical claims with reproducible evidence.
- Correct mistakes openly.
- Preserve attribution.
- Respect security and privacy boundaries.
- Avoid public disclosure of unresolved vulnerabilities.
- Keep reviews focused on project impact.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect review.
- Accept that strong claims invite strong verification.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following is not permitted in project spaces:

- Targeted insults, threats, stalking, or harassment.
- Coordinated pile-ons or instructions to contact an individual.
- Publishing personal information.
- Identity-based abuse.
- Deliberately false claims about contributors or upstream maintainers.
- Impersonation or misleading affiliation.
- Sabotage of evidence, tests, provenance, or review records.
- Using project infrastructure to continue unrelated social-media arguments.
- Retaliation against a contributor for making a good-faith correction.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when conduct threatens the work or participants.

Technical disagreement is not harassment. Personal abuse is not technical disagreement.

## Core standard

**Be willing to be wrong. Be rigorous enough to become right.**
