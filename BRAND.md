# Brand

## Name

**LATTICEWORK**

The name carries two meanings:

1. A structure made from visible, connected pieces.
2. A lattice that performs real work.

The name should always appear as `LATTICEWORK` in major headings and `Latticework` in normal prose.

## Tagline

**A lattice that works.**

## Repository description

> Compatibility-conscious reengineering of FreeLattice. Preserve the heart, rebuild the shape, and prove every claim.

## One-paragraph description

LATTICEWORK is an independent, MIT-licensed reengineering of FreeLattice focused on behavior preservation, explicit architecture, reproducible verification, local-first operation, and reliable human-agent collaboration across interrupted contexts.

## Visual identity direction

The visual identity should communicate:

- Connected structure without mystical opacity.
- Precision without sterile corporate design.
- Visible nodes, edges, layers, and boundaries.
- Dark technical surfaces with high-contrast structural accents.
- Evidence, instrumentation, and inspectability.

## Distinction rules

LATTICEWORK must not copy or imitate:

- Upstream logos.
- Dragon or Fractal Family branding.
- Upstream contributor identity marks.
- Upstream slogans.
- Any visual presentation likely to imply endorsement or official continuation.

## Naming patterns

Recommended:

- `LATTICEWORK Core`
- `LATTICEWORK Runtime`
- `LATTICEWORK Compatibility Harness`
- `LATTICEWORK Audit`
- `LATTICEWORK Baseline`
- `LATTICEWORK Release`

Avoid:

- `FreeLattice Alpha`
- `Official FreeLattice`
- `FreeLattice 2`
- Names that imply the upstream project approved or commissioned the fork
