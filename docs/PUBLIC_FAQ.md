# Public FAQ

## What is LATTICEWORK?

LATTICEWORK is an independent, compatibility-conscious reengineering of FreeLattice focused on explicit architecture, reproducible verification, local-first operation, and reliable human-agent collaboration.

## Is this an official FreeLattice project?

No. LATTICEWORK is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by FreeLattice or its maintainers.

## Why fork instead of starting from nothing?

A direct fork preserves provenance, license history, and a visible baseline. It also makes behavior-preserving comparison possible.

## Why not only contribute to upstream?

Some fixes may be suitable for upstream. The broader architecture, verification system, governance, and roadmap are intentionally independent.

## Is this a personal attack?

No. The project began after a public technical disagreement, but the repository evaluates software claims through pinned source, tests, measurements, and reproducible evidence. Project spaces do not permit personal harassment.

## Are you claiming FreeLattice has no value?

No. LATTICEWORK exists because the upstream project contains enough ambition and worthwhile behavior to make serious reengineering useful.

## Are you claiming compaction is lossless?

No. Compaction is lossy. LATTICEWORK designs critical truth into durable repository surfaces so that the loss is bounded and recoverable.

## Did you make the code smaller by deleting features?

That claim must be answered by the compatibility matrix, divergence record, and pinned comparison. Smaller source without preserved behavior is not a victory.

## Is LATTICEWORK a drop-in replacement?

Not until `docs/COMPATIBILITY.md` says so for a defined release and supported scope.

## How can I verify the claims?

Use the commands, pinned commits, raw artifacts, and clean-room procedures linked from `docs/CLAIMS_LEDGER.md` and `docs/METRICS_AND_BENCHMARKS.md`.

## Can upstream corrections change the audit?

Yes. Factual corrections are welcome. Claims will be corrected, superseded, or withdrawn when evidence requires it.
